Building this site

September 9, 2026 · Week 1

First class assignment. Set up the repository, got it on GitHub Pages, and picked type and color.

What I changed

I kept the structure and changed everything in custom.css. The body font is Inter and the headings are Instrument Serif, which is a lot more contrast between the two than I expected to want. The green came from a photo of the sign outside Bombadill's.

What broke

My stylesheet did nothing for about twenty minutes. I had the two <link> tags in the wrong order, so simple.css was loading second and overwriting all of my colors. Order matters.

The second thing: I put the @import for the fonts halfway down the file. It has to be the first line.
